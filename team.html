THE PEOPLE BEHIND CYCLECARE

Meet the Team

Meet the students who brought CycleCare to life through research, coding, collaboration, and a shared goal of making menstrual health information more accessible to teenager girls!

Ananya Mangla

Project Lead and Coder

Senior @ The Academy for Mathematics, Science & Engineering

Contributions

The idea for CycleCare came from recognizing a lack in menstrual health education in schools, as many girls rely on social media or their peers for information about periods rather than medically accurate sources.

To offset this deficit, I organized and guided a team of my peers who collaborated on coding, research, and web-design to create CycleCare. Throughout the process, I contributed to the research by finding commonly asked period questions by teenage girls, and I also coded the entire backend and frontend of CycleCare's website.

My Hope for CycleCare

“I hope CycleCare closes the gap in menstrual education by helping teenage girls take care of themselves with confidence instead of fear.”

Melanie Janania

Researcher for Mood and Physical Changes

Morris Hills High School 2026 High School Graduate

Contributions

I assessed numerous questions that came in regards to mood and physical changes for girls who were experiencing their periods and needed answers.

I made sure to seek reliable sources online for my information and compare multiple answers to make sure my questions gave as much information as possible. I also made sure to ask around for questions which best fit the categories I researched for more than one source.
